    How Neo uses this
    
          
    
            Nothing is embedded or searched by similarity. Neo matches the words of
            a question against each entry's code, name and
            aliases, as whole phrases. Only the entries that match are shown
            to the language model, and it is told to use no other campus facts.
          
    
          
    
            When the model host is away, which is most of the time, Neo answers from
            the matched entry by itself. So a place people cannot find by the words
            they use is a place Neo does not know.
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Format
    
          
    
            
    rooms.yaml is a list, one entry per place people ask for.
              Fields marked * are required. Anything not in this table is rejected, so a typo cannot silently lose data.
    
            
    
              
    
                
    FieldWhat to putExample
    
                
    code *Exactly as signposted on the door. Unique. For places with no number, make a short one.CS-204, LIB
    
                
    name *The official name, in full.Computer Science Lab 2
    
                
    block *Just the block's letter or number, spelled the same everywhere. Neo adds the word "block".B
    
                
    floor *A whole number. 0 is the ground floor, -1 the basement.2
    
                
    typeclassroom, lab, office or facility. Lets "which labs are in B block" filter.lab
    
                
    wingThe wing or side, if the block has them.East
    
                
    departmentThe department that owns it.CSE
    
                
    aliasesEvery other way people say it: short names, old names, nicknames. A list.["cs lab 2", "programming lab"]
    
                
    landmarksPermanent things beside it that a visitor can see. The first is spoken. A list.["opposite the lift"]
    
                
    directionsHow to walk there from the reception desk, said the way you would say it. Wins over the graph.Take the stairs on your left up one floor.
    
                
    notesOne short sentence: opening hours, who to ask.Open 9 am to 5 pm on weekdays.
    
                
    nodeOptional. The nearest node in graph.yaml, for directions worked out from the graph.b2_lab_corridor
    
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            
    graph.yaml is optional. It describes walking routes as nodes
              (places: id, kind, block, floor) joined by
              edges (from, to, instruction, and optionally
              distance_m, kind, reverse_instruction). Directions start at the node
              with id reception and follow the shortest route. An edge is one-way unless it has a
              reverse_instruction, because "go up the stairs" is wrong walked backwards.
              If you only need directions to a few places, a room's directions text is simpler.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    coverage.yaml says how much of each block has been entered, so Neo can
              be honest. complete: every room on the listed floors is in rooms.yaml, so a miss there means the
              room does not exist. partial: some rooms are entered. not_surveyed: nothing yet, and Neo says
              "I haven't learned C block yet" instead of guessing.
    
          
    
          
    
            Example
          
    
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Writing data Neo can find
    
          
      
            
    1. Add the places people actually ask for, not just classrooms: the library, canteen,
              washrooms, admin office, fee counter, principal's office, placement cell, parking. Use
              type: facility for these.
      2. 
            
    2. Aliases are how Neo finds things. Add the words students really use: "canteen",
              "cafeteria", "mess"; "seminar hall", "auditorium"; old names from before a renaming. Each alias
              is matched as a whole phrase anywhere in the question, word for word, so add a plural too if people
              say one ("chemistry labs").
      3. 
            
    3. Do not add spellings of the code as aliases. "CS-204", "cs204", "C S 204" and a spoken
              "c s two oh four" are all matched already. Do add a spoken form Neo mishears, once you have seen
              it in Try a question or on the Audio tab.
      4. 
            
    4. Avoid general aliases. One word like "lab", "room" or "office" matches almost every
              question. Say "chemistry lab", not "lab". The checker warns about these.
      5. 
            
    5. Sharing an alias is fine when it is really shared. If every floor has a "washroom", give
              them all that alias. Neo will ask which one the person means. Never give one room another room's code.
      6. 
            
    6. Be consistent with blocks and floors. Always "B", never "B block" or "Block-B". Floors are
              numbers, 0 for the ground floor.
      7. 
            
    7. Landmarks must be visible and permanent: "opposite the lift", "next to the notice board".
              Not "near the red poster". Put the most useful one first; it is the one spoken.
      8. 
            
    8. Write directions from the reception desk, for the ear. Short steps, left and right, stairs
              or lift, floor changes: "Go past the notice board, take the lift to the second floor, it's the
              first door on your right."
      9. 
            
    9. Keep fields short. A small model reads them and a speaker says them. One sentence of notes,
              no paragraphs, no URLs or phone-number lists.
      10. 
            
    10. Keep coverage honest. Mark a block complete only when every room on those
              floors is entered. Until then use partial, or Neo will tell someone a real room does
              not exist.
      11. 
            
    11. Test before you save. Try each new entry with two or three phrasings. If the model is
              shown the wrong entry, or none, fix the aliases, not the prompt.
      12.

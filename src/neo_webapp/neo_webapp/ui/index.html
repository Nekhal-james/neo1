Detection & engagement

Set the camera source to webapp and start the camera on the Sources tab.
Show your palm — hand up, forearm vertical — and hold it for about half a second to engage. Your raised elbow has to be in shot: from a laptop camera that means sitting back about a metre, because the model will not guess an elbow below the frame. The lock follows you, survives the detector dropping a frame, and releases on its own when you leave the frame or turn your back. If it will not lock, Palm check says which part of the pose it cannot see.
